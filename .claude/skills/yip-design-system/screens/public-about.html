How Yip works

We find the good flights, so you don't have to.

Yip is a curated travel club for Lithuania and the Baltics — not a search engine. A real person finds, checks and publishes genuinely good deals. You just subscribe and book.

1

Our scanner watches fares

Around the clock, across Vilnius, Kaunas, Riga and nearby airports — so you never have to search.

2

Yip flags the real gems

It scores each fare against months of history and groups it into travel moments, like "September sun".

3

A human checks every deal

We verify the price, dates and catches by hand. If it's not genuinely good, it doesn't go out.

4

You book directly

Tap through and book with the airline or travel site. Yip just points you to the good ones, fast.

Good to know

You don't search — we do

There's no route/date search box. We publish the deals worth your time.

Deals can expire fast

Great fares sell out in hours. We mark the urgent ones and email subscribers first.

Booking happens externally

We link you out to the airline or travel site — Yip never takes your payment.
